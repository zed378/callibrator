`services/migration.service.js:87`).

Effect: `POST /sessions/user/:userId/revoke-all` returns **403 for every caller who can reach it**, and a
super admin can revoke or delete only their **own** sessions. The unit tests mock
`role: { name: "SUPER_ADMIN" }` (`tests/controllers/session.controller.test.js:400,484,522`), which is
the "mock invents the contract" trap. `sessions.e2e` does not exercise revoke-all. This is the V-15
shape with a live consequence (P6-12 "revocation that revokes"). **Needs an A-id and a fix with a
fail-before test.**
