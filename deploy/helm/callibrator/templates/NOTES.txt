────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  SECRETS — back these up separately from the cluster AND the database
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CERT_SIGNING_SECRET   losing it: EVERY issued certificate permanently fails
                        public verification; the key cannot be re-derived
  ENCRYPT_KEY           losing it: every tenant private key and every stored
                        storage credential becomes undecryptable
  KMS_MASTER_KEY        losing it: every wrapped tenant secret (SSO
                        certificates, OIDC, Stripe and webhook secrets)
                        becomes undecryptable

  A restore that recovers the database and loses these produces a system that
  starts cleanly and is permanently broken. None is practically rotatable.

  The recovery drill must assert that a PRE-INCIDENT CERTIFICATE STILL
  VERIFIES — that is the check which catches a lost-secrets restore before
  weeks pass. See docs/ARCHITECTURE/09-DISASTER-RECOVERY.md.
