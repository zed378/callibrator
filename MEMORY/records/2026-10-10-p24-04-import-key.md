## Deviations (ADR-133 Am. 4)

1. The key is provisioned by the import, not by the operator through `POST /api-keys`: nobody ever holds its secret, so "never used over HTTP" is a property.
2. A NULL user's snapshot has a name (`Upstream import`): § 9.1's `name: null` fails the D-27 shape.
3. A mapped-but-missing user is refused, not attributed to the key.
