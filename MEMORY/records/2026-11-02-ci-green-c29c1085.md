## The three failures on Windows, and why they were the environment

The first `--with=all` ran on Windows at the same time as the full unit run, and gave 65 of 68, 497 passed, 20 failed. None of the three is a defect in the product:

- **`dbc`, `dbd`:** load, but a real weakness under it. `beforeAll` runs all 148 migrations, and these two had the tightest budget of the suites that do, 120 s against 180 s to 900 s. Under load they reached migration 0128 and 0133 and timed out, progressing, not hung. Alone: `dbc` 4/4 in 17 s, `dbd` 6/6 in 27 s. Their budget is now 300 s; a CI runner could overrun 120 s the same way.
- **`am3`:** the base tree's `cls-hooked` was missing. `provisionAm3Modules`' cache `%TEMP%/callibrator-live-am3-base` had lost its files, all at 04:27:17–25 UTC, a minute after the runner had checked them. What deleted them is not established: a second full unit run left the cache intact, so the unit suite is not the reliable cause, and the other session was active in the same temp directory that day. With the cache rebuilt: `am3` 10/10. On Linux: passed.
- **Failed before that, and also the environment:** the first guard run had no `backend/.env`, and the first MQTT container got a Git Bash path rewrite (`MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` is the cure, as the 2026-10-29 record says).

One further load timeout in the unit run: `batchJob.service.test.js` "skips the queue entirely when BATCH_JOBS_INLINE=true", 10 s. Alone it passed (with five other suites, 65/65).
