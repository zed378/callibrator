# P25-02: Q-4, Q-14 and Q-28's missing files added to the data-quality report

**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Card:** P25-02 · **Status:** PARTIAL (Q-28's unreferenced files and the orphan, file and user sections still not built) · **Decision:** ADR-129 Am. 12 item 9 · **Spec refs:** `docs/UPSTREAM/03` § 7 (Q-4, Q-14, Q-28), 05 § 7

## Hook

Three of the report's findings answered `manual` with "NOT AUTOMATED". They are now computed per
facility, from what the transform already wrote. No grant or contract change was needed.

## What changed

[`backend/src/services/upstreamDataQuality.service.ts`](../../backend/src/services/upstreamDataQuality.service.ts)
computes three more findings per facility:

| Finding | Counts | Facility from |
|---|---|---|
| **Q-4** | The run's IPM session rows (four session tables) quarantined `no_device`: their QR matches no imported device. Their results follow as `session_not_imported` and are not counted again | The staged `id_client`, through the facility step's `mst_faskes` mapping. A quarantined row has no mapping of its own |
| **Q-14** | Calibrations dated before their device's `inventoried_on`. Flagged as plausible (calibrated before registration) | The calibration row |
| **Q-28**, missing half | Photos a mapped device names whose file is missing (`file_missing`, per folder) | The device's facility |

Q-28 has a second half that is **not counted**: files on disk that no row names. That needs the
manifest. A facility with no missing photo therefore answers `manual`, never `pass`, and the note
says why.

The same change touched:

- the OpenAPI description, `openapi.json` and the frontend `schema.d.ts`;
- the header of the `upstreamDataQuality` contract (a comment only);
- the as-built lists in `docs/UPSTREAM/05` § 7, `docs/BACKEND/13`, the runbook, `PHASE-25`,
  `PROGRESS` and ADR-129 Am. 12.
