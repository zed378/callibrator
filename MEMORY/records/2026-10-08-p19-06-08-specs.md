## Decisions (agent, under the owner's delegation — the owner may revise)

- **IPM report (ADR-126 Am. 2):**
  - The submitted **session is the issued record**; the report is its document. **No certificate row** — `docs/UPSTREAM/09` § 2.5 and `02` F-58 proposed one; that would have made a bound technician's submit issue a certificate (the marker guard refuses any certificate action but read), imposed an approval step the IPM does not have, and duplicated one fact across paranoid rows with a global number.
  - **Number** `IPM-<facility code>-<YYYYMMDD>-<NNN>`, sequenced per tenant, **facility** and local day, so a facility learns only its own volume; one per submitted session.
  - **Verification requires the 192-bit token**: one byte-identical 404 for a wrong, absent or mismatched token and an unknown number — no minimal verdict (no IPM report was ever printed without a token).
  - **Content hash `ipm-report-v1`**, canonical payload in contracts, **stored** at submit and recomputed at every read; a mismatch is shown, printed, logged and alerted.
  - **`issuer_snapshot`** at submit; the logo is live and unhashed (stated on the page).
  - **Signatures** in a new facility-scoped, append-only `inspection_session_signatures`, not in the provider-internal `e_signature_records`; the technician signs **after** the submit, online, with a re-entered credential — never from the offline outbox; the facility's `FACILITY MAINTENANCE` countersigns after it, never the submitter, only with the tenant setting `ipm.countersignEnabled`; the wet-signature line always stays printable; no signature gates anything.
  - Bound HT and FM get `esignature` W in the ceiling, reaching only the one marked route (N-5).
  - Rendering is audited through the document read with `render=pdf|print` (an `EXPORT` row before the document is sent).
  - The signature route joins the frontend client's credential endpoints, so a wrong signing password neither refreshes nor ends the session.
- **Offline capture (ADR-127 Am. 1):**
  - Worker **scope `/field`**, not `/`; the field app is **one document** (`/field`, screens as client state), because App Router navigations need the server.
  - `public/sw.js` generated before the build, versioned, outside the proxy matcher, with its own worker CSP; it never intercepts `/api/**`.
  - Install pre-caches the shell's assets atomically; the app warms lazy modules; a missing module degrades one feature only.
  - Per-record AES-GCM with the record's slot as AAD; the working set in encrypted pages decrypted into memory — no plaintext index of tenant values.
  - The sync engine **plans** the minimal op from the local capture and **freezes** key and body before sending.
  - Purge on a **failed refresh** and on `SCOPE_LOSS_CODES` — never on a raw 401/403 —, on 72 h by device clock and by server `Date` (AM-24), and on a changed `scopeFingerprint` from `/auth/verify` (AM-26); the outbox survives every purge.
  - One field user per profile (AM-23, OQ-10) with an audited administrator wipe (`POST /field/wipes`).
  - `camera=(self)` on `/field` only; iOS offline mode requires the installed app.
  - Budgets: `/` unchanged; `/field` ≤ 170 KB brotli; the dashboard ≤ +1 KB (a lazily loaded guard behind a `localStorage` flag).

## For the owner or SME (none blocks a build; recommendations given)

- Should a facility be able to **require** the IPSRS countersignature before its own staff see a report? Recommended **no**: the record exists at submit, and a pending countersignature is printed, not hidden.
- Should the report print the technician's **employee number**? Recommended **no**.
- Is the working-set cap (2,000 devices per facility; a tenant may raise it to 5,000) right for the largest real facility? To be read as an aggregate in the dry run (P24-05).
- May a provider technician take **more than three** facilities offline at once? Recommended **no**: a lost phone's exposure grows with it.
