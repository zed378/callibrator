## What was fixed in it

- **`"deepmerge": "^4.3.2"` was removed from `overrides`.** No such version exists (the registry's last is 4.3.1), so `npm ls` reported `deepmerge@4.3.1 invalid`. The audit's addendum said that fix "is `>=4.3.2`"; that version was never published. The advisory stays, as one low in a dev-only tree.
- **`tests/utils/checkMenu.test.ts`** still required the deleted file, so it would have failed. It is deleted with the file.
- **Two stale allow-list entries** for the deleted file were removed: `noConsole.a42` and the `no-console` block in `eslint.config.js`.
- **`webhook.emitCap.w17` and `webhook.delivery.a10` still faked `create`.** emitEvent then threw on `bulkCreate`, and those suites failed (3 of 5 and 6 of 7). Their doubles now fake `bulkCreate`. w17's "a row for every webhook" now asserts one `bulkCreate` of 5 rows, not 5 `create`s. These are the same facts, written in one statement. `--findRelatedTests` misses w17 because it loads the service under `jest.isolateModules`.
- **`isLiveSession` was removed:** it was exported and called nowhere.
