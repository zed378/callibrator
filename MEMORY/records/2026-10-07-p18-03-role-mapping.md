## What surprised me (findings that changed the design)

1. **The route marker alone leaves the menu wrong.** Under ADR-102 the sidebar and buttons read the same effective permission as `dynamicAccess`; a bound `HEALTHCARE ADMIN` keeps its role's `users`/`api-keys`/`storage` write grants, so its menu would offer administration pages that all 403. → a **bound menu ceiling inside `effectivePermission`** (ADR-124 Am. 1 § 1), which also stops a per-user override widening a bound user.
2. **The technical roles cannot write devices or calibration today.** `TECHNICIAN`, `HEALTHCARE TECHNICIAN` and `FACILITY MAINTENANCE` hold `equipment` read only (UI research 03 F4, open as its Q2). The upstream's commonest write — a technician registering a device — has no default grant here. Widening it changes every tenant, so it is an owner decision: **UD-4 (b)**, added with a recommendation (yes).
3. **`equipment` is a parent menu whose read reaches six child slugs** (`calibration`, `certificate`, `maintenance`, `calibration-scheduler`, `reports`, `predictive-maintenance`). Placing `ipm` and `ipm-templates` under it gives every reader IPM/catalogue read and the level-8 admins proposal write with no extra rows — exactly what P19-01 wanted.
4. **`POST /attachments` (upload) is gated by `equipment` *read*** and accepts standalone types (`generic`, `ticket`, `post`): a bound `ROOM USER` could upload into the tenant's storage. Marked for bound users only with a `boundGate` (own-facility device/IPM resource, write on its slug).
5. **The threat model's AM-11 would forbid the bound technician's IPM capture**: it refuses the marker on `denyPlatformAuthoring`, which is ADR-052's Part 11 authorship guard and will sit on the IPM writes. Refined: not a criterion; any non-read `certificate` action and the ceiling cover the lab-issuance routes it aimed at.
6. **Infrastructure reads made on a bound user's behalf would silently fail** under the deny branch — tenant settings (the IPM interval, time zone, storage driver), the KMS key, quotas, and the subject's own rows for a GDPR export. Putting them on `FACILITY_READABLE` would make them readable through any marked route; they get reviewed `skipFacilityScope` calls instead (G-13).
7. **Two per-user tables are not tenant-scoped at all** (`webauthn_credentials`, `notification_states`), so they need no `FACILITY_READABLE` entry.

## Decisions (ADR-124 Amendment 1; alternatives and bad implications in `MEMORY/DECISIONS.md`)

Bound role set confirmed (HA, HT, FM, RU); **no new role, `ROLE_LEVELS` unchanged** · bound menu ceiling in the effective permission (every bound role reads devices, records, certificates, work orders, IPM, the catalogue; HT may also write `calibration` — only if UD-4 (b) grants it — and `ipm`; nothing administrative) · **a bound `HEALTHCARE ADMIN` is read-only** in this group · the marker is one reviewed constant (`FACILITY_ACCESSIBLE_ROUTES`, `kind`/`reason`/`selfParam`/`boundGate`) and one middleware; the guard refuses `superAdminOnly`, tenant-tier `rbac`/`checkRoleLevel`, `abac(TENANT_PERMISSIONS.*)`, an administrative slug deny-list, any non-read certificate action, any action outside the ceiling, and public routes · `FACILITY_READABLE`: `ClientFacility` (own row), `Session`, `Notification`, `ConsentRecord`, `DsarRequest` (own user), signature records only after UD-17 · three slugs `ipm`, `ipm-templates` (under `equipment`), `client-facilities` (Management › Organization); `ipm` write to `TECHNICIAN`, `HEALTHCARE TECHNICIAN`, `FACILITY MAINTENANCE` · `my-permissions` gains `facilityBound`; `GET /client-facilities/mine` is the bound user's own-facility read · threat-model OQ-2 (bound technicians create/edit devices; QR not settable or a 409 naming nothing), OQ-8 (dashboard only with its two-facility test and G-20), OQ-9 (per-user tables yes, tickets later) answered.

**Route list (spec § 8):** marked — the self/identity routes (S-1 … S-8), device list/get and (HT) create/edit, calibration-record reads, attachment reads and the narrowed upload, certificate reads (list, get, document, PDF link), work-order reads, rooms after UD-10, dashboard under OQ-8; target routes N-1 (catalogue reads), N-2/N-3 (IPM reads and HT capture), N-5 (signatures, UD-17), N-6, N-8, N-9. Not marked: every write of calibration records, certificates, work orders; device delete/restore/reinstate/bulk import; IPM void; reports, search, AI, scheduler, predictive maintenance, IoT, stock, vendors, QMS, kanban, tickets, and every administrative route.

**Separation of duties (spec § 11):** IPM draft/submit by its creator; correction by any `ipm` writer in scope; void only by an unbound tenant administrator (no rule against its own submitter — a retraction with a reason, not an approval); performer signature by the submitter only; IPSRS countersignature by a `FACILITY MAINTENANCE` of the session's facility who is **not** the submitter (UD-17, cells marked); certificates unchanged (ADR-101); no self-binding by an administrator.

**For the owner (Phase 12 § 3, UD-4):** (b) `calibration` write for `TECHNICIAN`/`HEALTHCARE TECHNICIAN` by default — recommended yes; (c) facility user management by a bound admin — recommended no in this group. UD-17 and UD-10 cells are marked in the matrix; AM-15 (JIT/SCIM users inactive in multi-facility tenants) stays with P21-09 for the owner's confirmation.
