# P25-01: R-9 automated — imported accounts against their upstream group's role

**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Card:** P25-01 · **Status:** PARTIAL (R-8, RC-F4 and R-12 still `manual`) · **Decision:** ADR-129 Am. 12 item 6 · **Spec refs:** `docs/UPSTREAM/05` § 7 (R-9), P18-01 § 2.1 (group → role)

## Hook

05 § 7 states R-9 as "members per group against users per (tenant, role)". That count compares
two totals. Like R-3 … R-6 earlier today, it is done row by row instead: every imported account
must hold a role its staged group gives. Equal rows give equal per-role totals, and a swapped pair
of accounts cannot hide behind equal totals.

## What changed

| | |
|---|---|
| [`backend/src/services/upstreamReconciliation.service.ts`](../../backend/src/services/upstreamReconciliation.service.ts) | `userRoles`: the run's staged `users` joined to `id_map`, then to `users` in the provider tenant and their role. It counts those whose role is one their staged membership's group gives. `GROUP_ROLE_PAIRS` is P18-01 § 2.1 written as literals (admin → CALIBRATOR ADMIN, user → TECHNICIAN, client → ROOM USER or HEALTHCARE ADMIN, teknisi_client → HEALTHCARE TECHNICIAN), bound as two arrays. The group name is folded as users.ts folds it (`lower(btrim(name))`). If the membership or group tables were not staged, `matching` is 0, so any landed account fails |
| `admin.openapi.ts`, `openapi.json`, frontend `schema.d.ts` | The description: R-9 at the run level |
| `docs/UPSTREAM/05` § 7, `docs/BACKEND/13`, runbook, `PHASE-25`, ADR-129 Am. 12 | The as-built lists |

The check is **run-level**, because provider staff (groups admin and user) have no facility.

**Not checked:** which of HEALTHCARE ADMIN and ROOM USER a `client` received. users.ts gives
HEALTHCARE ADMIN to its facility's earliest-created active client, with ties broken by the lowest
upstream id. Re-deriving that would repeat the step's own logic. So a client given the wrong one
of the two passes R-9; the note says so.
