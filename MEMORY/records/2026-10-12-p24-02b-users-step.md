**Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Card:** P24-02b (the second part of P24-02) · **ADR:** ADR-129 Amendment 4 · **Status:** DONE. The transform stays **not available** (409) until steps 3 … 12 are built.

## What was built

| | |
|---|---|
| `transform/users.ts` (step 2) | Sources `users`, `auth_groups`, `auth_groups_users` (keyed `group_id:user_id`) and `trx_mapping_user_client`. P18-01-02 § 2.2: `admin` → CALIBRATOR ADMIN and `user` → TECHNICIAN, both unbound. `client` is bound to its one mapped facility; per facility, the earliest-created active client (ties: lowest id) is HEALTHCARE ADMIN and the others ROOM USER. `teknisi_client` → bound HEALTHCARE TECHNICIAN. Inactive accounts are imported inactive. There is one unusable bcrypt per run with `must_change_password`. The e-mail is lower-cased; a taken username gets a suffix; `fullname` is split on its last space. A changed user has its names and active flag updated, never its identity or binding. Use-only rows map to the user, or role, they decided |
| Quarantine | `user_without_group`, `group_conflict`, `facility_mapping_ambiguous`, `facility_not_imported`, `email_in_use`, `unknown_group`, `user_not_imported` (the five new ones are in contracts and the 0135 CHECK) |
