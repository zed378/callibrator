## Decisions (agent, under the owner's delegation — the owner may revise)

- **IPM (ADR-126 Am. 1):**
  - `client_ref` is unique **per creator** and resolved in the caller's context (OQ-6, AM-16).
  - The visit number is **max + 1** at the chain's first submit. Imported visits are renumbered by date; the upstream value (a 0/1 flag + 1) is kept as `legacy_visit_number`, so it does not break the unique index.
  - Triggers, not grants, hold the drafts. Both triggers admit `client_facility_id` only under `callibrator.facility_move`, and 0117's facility functions gain a `result` branch.
  - Neither table is paranoid. Environment readings are results, not header columns.
  - Device, facility and performer snapshots are taken at submit, including the device's last and next calibration dates (`09` L-1).
  - Side effects apply **on entry only**, as a Completed Preventative order plus UD-17's three effects. A correction applies only the delta. A void cancels only the visit's own Preventative order and the derived calibration request; nothing else is reversed silently.
  - `denyApiKey` is on every IPM write. There is no prefill route; there is one open root draft per device per technician.
  - Idempotency stores a scope fingerprint and a resource reference, **never a body** (AM-25). `IdempotencyKey` joins `FACILITY_READABLE`.
- **Devices (ADR-132):**
  - The QR is normalised with the **tenant's** prefix and digit settings and is unique per tenant over every row.
  - **Only provider staff write the QR** (OQ-2 finalised). FT-50 is closed, because no QR 409 reaches a bound user.
  - The lookup is registered before `/:id` and is marked (N-13).
  - `condition` is separate from `status`.
  - Rooms are `warehouses` of `kind = room`, with a floor, inside the device's facility, held by a trigger rather than a composite FK, because existing devices point at stores. Rooms are found or created from the device form. `warehouse` read joins the bound ceiling and A-9 is marked.
  - `attachments.purpose` allows one live front and one live serial-plate photo per device. `folder` keeps its storage meaning; there is no `certificates-legacy` folder.
  - Photos are not mandatory at the API.
  - A multi-facility tenant's device create names its facility.
  - The device move clears or re-targets the room.
- **Calibration dates (ADR-133):**
  - The next due date is **derived from the latest effective record** on create, correction and void, with its source tracked.
  - The quick entry `POST /calibration-devices/:id/calibration-dates` writes an `external_date` record with no file. It is unmarked, because it is provider work (N-10).
  - `performer_snapshot` is written at insert only. This narrows ADR-124 Am. 2 § 7, which rejected a back-fill (that rule is kept).
  - "Calibration due" is computed at read, including the IPM request flag.
  - There is one recap read (`dateField`, `latestOnly`, `entryKind`).
  - Imported dates name the person when resolvable, else the per-tenant import key. No laboratory is inferred, and future dates are quarantined.
  - UD-8's interim rule holds by construction: imported devices get no interval and no stated due date, so nothing is derived.
