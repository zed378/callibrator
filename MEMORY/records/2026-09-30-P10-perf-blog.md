# 2026-09-30 — P10-13 (performance part): the public pages' JavaScript, and blog/news on the public surface

**Card:** P10-13 (performance and the AC-7 budget; the live E2E, keyboard/NVDA walks and release checklist remain open) · **ADR:** ADR-098 **Amendment 2** · **Amends:** doc 20 §13 AC-7 (measurement), doc 19 §11 (blog/news chrome) · **Follows:** the Phase 10 frontend lead's as-built record (`2026-09-30-P10-frontend-as-built.md`), which found AC-5/6/7 unmet and named the cause.

## What was wrong

The root layout (`src/app/layout.tsx`) rendered `ThemeProvider`, `TenantBrandingProvider`, `AuthInitializer` and `ToastContainer`. A client component imported by a layout is in the bundle of every page under it, so every public page downloaded and hydrated them — and axios and the auth store behind them (a 16.2 KB brotli chunk of axios alone). `AuthInitializer` also called the backend's verify endpoint from public pages. Four smaller costs sat on top:

- `MessagesProvider` imported the full English dictionary as its fallback, and `useLoginForm` / `FirstPasswordChangeForm` imported it for test-only exports: ~21 KB raw in every sign-in-family bundle.
- `i18n/apiErrors` imported axios for `isAxiosError`, so the verification page (which uses `fetch`) would have carried axios on its own.
- lucide-react 1.x's `Icon` is `"use client"`: every lucide icon, even in a server component, is client JavaScript, and the root not-found boundary put that runtime on every page.
- Turbopack emitted `next/link` twice on `/verify/*` (once in the not-found boundary's chunk group, once in the page's).
- Blog and news still used `LandingLayout` (Lenis + GSAP, an 85.6 KB brotli chunk).

P10-13 still needs the measurement on a quiet machine or the VM before AC-5/6 can be judged.
