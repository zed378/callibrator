## Found while writing (each is recorded where it belongs)

- The public verification page is **not excluded from indexing** (`frontend/src/app/robots.ts` does not cover `/verify`; the page sets no `robots` metadata), contrary to doc 14. → P10-08.
- `docs/UI-UX/08` listed four verification verdicts; the page computes six. → amended.
- The WebAuthn login endpoints need a session (`webauthn.route.js:13` `router.use(auth)`), confirmed in code; registration already requires discoverable credentials (`webauthn.service.ts:126`). → P10-10.
- `tenant.service.js#createTenant` owns its transaction; approval needs an outer-transaction option. → P10-05.
- A tenant-scoped model is detected by a `tenantId`/`tenant_id` attribute (`tenantScope.util.ts#tenantKeyOf`), so the access-request table names its tenant link `provisionedTenantId`.

## A mistake corrected before it shipped

An early draft of doc 20 §9 and of A-291 said `POST /auth/send-otp` enumerates accounts, from reading `auth.service.js#requestOTP` alone (lines 686 vs 717 differ). The controller discards those messages and always sends one neutral text (`auth.controller.js:135`), as research 05 §5.6 says. Both were corrected; A-291 now records the real defect (successes never counted). Reading the service without the controller is the same mistake as trusting a mock.
